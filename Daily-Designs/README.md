Welcome to my **Daily Designs** workspace! 👋 

This repository is a living, breathing showcase of my journey through the 100-day [Daily UI](https://www.dailyui.co/) challenge. As I actively build my UI/UX portfolio, this space serves as a chronological record of my growth, experiments, and creative problem-solving.


If you love seeing the raw evolution of design skills, you are in the right place. **Dive into the folders and take a look around!** 👀

---

## 🚀 The Challenge

For 100 consecutive working days, I receive a unique, surprise design prompt in my inbox. The goal isn't just to make something that looks pretty—it's to rapidly ideate, focus on the user experience, and deliver a clean, functional interface within a tight timeframe.

---

## 🧠 My Creative Process

Here is how a project goes from a blank canvas to a finished file:

1. **📩 The Prompt:** Check the inbox for the day's Daily UI challenge.
2. **💡 Ideation:** Break down the core user problem and sketch out mental models.
3. **✨ Execution:** Bring the concept to life using **Figma**, focusing on visual hierarchy, typography, and accessibility.
